# Content gap analysis

Observed source routes currently cover homepage, services overview, about, why-us, contact, tracking, team, and chairman message. No independently routed service detail pages, trade lane pages, industry pages, guide library, or published case studies were found in the source route set.

| Gap | Intent | Lead potential | Recommendation | Verification |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| Air freight Qatar standalone page | Commercial | High | Explain cargo fit, booking/handling, airport-to-door options, limits and CTA | Verify partner/handling claims and actual operating process |
| Sea freight/FCL/LCL page | Commercial | High | Explain FCL vs LCL, consolidation, port process, documents and quote inputs | Confirm actual origins, carrier arrangements and service scope |
| Road freight/GCC page | Commercial | High | Clarify country coverage, border handling and FTL/LTL options | Sales/operations approval required |
| Customs coordination page | Transactional | High | Explain what Argus does vs licensed broker/authority responsibilities | Confirm legal scope and documentation guidance |
| Warehousing/consolidation page | Commercial | High | Describe actual storage, handling and inventory systems | Confirm locations, capability, certifications, capacity and security |
| Door-to-door/China lane | Transactional | High | Describe pickup/consolidation/customs/delivery and cargo constraints | Confirm origin cities, methods, transit variables, fees and incoterms |
| India/Turkey/UAE trade lanes | Transactional | High | Create only lanes with real shipment options and unique process information | Validate operational service lane one at a time |
| Import documents / customs guide | Informational | Medium/high | Expert-reviewed checklist with links to official Qatar authority sources | Legal/customs review and update schedule |
| Air vs sea freight to Qatar | Comparison | Medium/high | Decision guide based on cargo, urgency, cost variables and not price promises | Operations approval |
| Case studies | Commercial trust | High | Show actual challenge, cargo, constraints, solution, outcome, service links | Client permission and source data |
| Industries | Commercial | Medium | Build only if material vertical experience is documented | Confirm actual served sectors and examples |

Do not create industry pages for construction, oil and gas, healthcare, food, retail or e-commerce based only on plausible market demand. Do not mass-produce lane pages from templates. Keep current pages intact until new content has unique value and URL overlap is assessed.
