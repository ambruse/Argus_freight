# International commercial keyword map

Research date: 2026-10-31. This is a qualitative intent map, not a volume/ranking report. Searches used English plus Arabic, Simplified Chinese and Turkish phrases; the search tool does not establish a controlled in-country Google SERP, rank, volume, competition or conversion rate. Native terms below are research seeds, not approved translations. Business approval is still required for expanded service/market claims and any new page.

## Language research seeds (not live translations)

Arabic: `الشحن الجوي`, `الشحن البحري`, `التخليص الجمركي`, `الشحن البري`, `تجميع الشحنات`. Chinese: `国际货运`, `海运整柜`, `海运拼箱`, `空运`, `中国到阿联酋物流`. Turkish: `Katar nakliye`, `hava kargo`, `denizyolu taşımacılığı`, `parsiyel`. Native commercial editors must validate usage, audience and terminology before keyword approval or translation. No Hindi route is proposed merely because India is a market.

## Cannibalization controls and measurement

One owner per intent: homepage = global company; country = local/network selection; service = mode/task; lane = directional shipment. FCL and LCL are service subtopics until query/lead evidence demonstrates a separate task requiring a distinct useful page. Link with descriptive topic anchors; retain titles and copy unless an evidenced mismatch is approved. Do not build city/service cross-products. Existing Qatar-focused service copy and global service titles merit scope review, not automatic keyword replacement.

With account access, compare GSC queries and landing URLs by country/device over matching periods. Investigate queries repeatedly splitting across competing pages, distinguishing legitimate market/language variation from intent duplication. Pair this with aggregated qualified enquiries by service/target market/landing page. Volumes, rank positions, qualified leads and actual cannibalization outcomes remain unknown.
