# 90-day SEO roadmap

## Days 1–15: repair the foundation

- Confirm canonical host and business identity facts; reconcile phone, address and hours.
- Connect GSC, GA4, GBP and Bing Webmaster Tools; export query/page/country/device and conversion baselines.
- Keep one homepage title; align canonical/Open Graph/schema hosts. Audit current deployment after source changes.
- Make private app routes noindex and exclude them from sitemap; validate public crawl resources remain accessible.
- Decide prerender/server rendering for public routes and define per-route metadata/content output.
- Test quote/contact submissions and add conversion events only after analytics ownership and consent setup are confirmed.

## Days 16–30: commercial architecture

- Give every current public route unique title, description, canonical and social metadata.
- Normalize one H1/page and meaningful headings without rewriting established copy unnecessarily.
- Strengthen service hub and internal links; select first dedicated air/sea/road/customs/warehouse pages after service fact-check.
- Reconcile NAP and GBP; use true hours/address only.
- Measure mobile/desktop LCP, INP, CLS and lab diagnostics. Prioritize real bottlenecks.

## Days 31–60: useful authority expansion

- Publish one or two operations-reviewed service pages at a time.
- Publish selected Qatar import/FCL-LCL/air-vs-sea resources based on Search Console/customer demand.
- Validate a first trade lane through sales/operations; do not template-scale.
- Gather case study evidence and permissions.
- Add breadcrumbs and matching data only when nested routes exist.

## Days 61–90: credibility and optimization

- Pitch useful Qatar/GCC logistics expertise to relevant publications and associations.
- Improve titles/snippets for high-impression/low-CTR queries using actual GSC data.
- Review positions 4–20 and conversion quality; refresh target pages based on query intent.
- Report organic qualified leads, quote starts/submissions, calls, email/WhatsApp and lead-to-opportunity outcomes.
- Recheck indexation, canonicals, sitemap, redirects and mobile CWV after deployments.
