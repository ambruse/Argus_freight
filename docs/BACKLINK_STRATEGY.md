# Digital PR and backlink strategy

Backlink counts and quality were not assessed because no backlink index or Search Console link export was available. The strategy should favor relevant, earned links and actual business relationships.

## Opportunity groups

- Qatar Chamber and legitimate business/trade associations, if membership exists.
- Logistics, freight and supply-chain publications through expert contributions and operational commentary.
- Customer/supplier/partner profiles where Argus is genuinely a named logistics partner.
- Verified certifications, memberships and carrier/agent networks, with permission to display.
- Original Qatar/GCC shipping explainers, import-document checklists, container/CBM resources, and case studies based on verified first-party information.
- Local business publications and event coverage for real company activity.

## Process and safeguards

Build a quarterly list of relevant publishers, pitch useful expertise, document placement/referral leads, and review links for relevance and accuracy. Do not buy links, use PBNs, mass-submit directories, automate comments, or require exact-match anchors. Separate paid sponsorships from editorial links and use appropriate disclosure/rel attributes.

Measure referring domains, relevant editorial placements, referral sessions, qualified lead assists and brand mentions—not raw link count alone.
