# 12-month SEO plan

## Q1 — Technical foundation and core Qatar commercial visibility

Resolve canonical and entity conflicts, ship unique metadata and reliable public rendering, exclude internal application pages, establish GSC/GA4/GBP/Bing baselines, verify conversion paths, and improve homepage/service/contact internal links. Publish the first service pages based on verified capability and query data.

## Q2 — Topical authority and validated trade lanes

Expand service depth, publish expert-reviewed Qatar shipping guides, pilot only the trade lanes Argus can substantiate, add relevant breadcrumbs, and document genuine case studies. Use Search Console query/page data to avoid cannibalization.

## Q3 — Digital PR and original logistics resources

Launch useful calculators/checklists where operational owners can maintain them; earn relevant mentions through associations, customers, partners and trade publications. Report lead quality, referral influence and relevant editorial coverage.

## Q4 — GCC/international expansion driven by evidence

Assess country/language demand from GSC and sales records. Localize Arabic only with professional translation and native search research. Add GCC pages only for genuine service presence and unique logistics guidance. Refresh content, entity facts, CWV and lead attribution.

## Governance

Quarterly review of canonicals, sitemap, robots, redirects, business facts, service claims, content accuracy, analytics consent and qualified lead attribution. Set numeric targets after a reliable baseline exists. Avoid guaranteeing rankings or expanding URLs to meet a publishing quota.
