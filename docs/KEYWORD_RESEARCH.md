Search volumes, difficulty scores and Argus rankings are marked **not verified** because no Keyword Planner, Search Console or licensed SEO dataset was connected. Do not interpret priority as measured volume.

## Prioritization

Initial order is based on fit with the current visible offer, not estimated demand: (1) Qatar freight forwarding/logistics hub, (2) air and sea freight, (3) road freight and customs, (4) warehousing/door-to-door, (5) validated trade lanes, (6) supporting educational content. Capture actual query, page, country, device, impression, CTR, position and conversion data from GSC/GA4 before selecting final primary keywords or volume-based targets.

## Competitor/SERP observations

General search results surfaced GAC Qatar, HSM Shipping, Aramex Qatar, DSV Qatar, QTRANS, Ottis Logistics, Cargo World Qatar and Delma Freight Services. Their indexed pages commonly foreground air/sea/land service breadth, warehouse presence, quote CTAs, local NAP, process details and service-level internal navigation. The sample is not a fixed ranking order and does not demonstrate keyword position or volume.
